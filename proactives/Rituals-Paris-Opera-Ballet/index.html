Rituals – Paris Opera Ballet

An Evening of Ballet at the Opéra Bastille

This winter, Paris welcomes an exceptional programme of dance at the Opéra Bastille. From 2 December 2026 to 2 January 2027, the Paris Opera Ballet presents Rituals, bringing together three works performed as part of one evening. Moving from classical elegance to bold contemporary expression, the programme offers three very different interpretations of movement, music and choreography.

Three Works, One Evening

SUITE EN BLANC Serge Lifar · Music by Édouard Lalo

A graceful celebration of classical technique and the elegance of the French ballet tradition.

BOLÉRO X Shahar Binyamini · Music by Maurice Ravel

A hypnotic and energetic work where movement builds alongside the unmistakable rhythm of Boléro.

THE RITE OF SPRING Pina Bausch · Music by Igor Stravinsky

Raw, powerful and intensely expressive, performed on an earth-covered stage in one of Bausch’s most celebrated works.

A Cultural Evening in Paris

A performance at the Opéra Bastille is a wonderful addition to a winter escape in Paris, whether paired with dinner in the city or made part of a longer stay. Rituals brings together the artistry of the Paris Opera Ballet and three remarkable works for an evening that goes far beyond a traditional night at the ballet. Allow us to secure your seats and shape the rest of your Paris evening around the performance.

Location: Opéra Bastille, Place de la Bastille, 75012 Paris
