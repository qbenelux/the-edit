Crans-Montana 2027: The Alpine World Ski Championships

The World’s Best Return to Crans-Montana

In February 2027, all eyes turn to Crans-Montana, Switzerland, as the FIS Alpine World Ski Championships return to the resort after forty years. For two weeks, the world’s leading skiers will compete for 11 world titles, bringing the excitement of elite alpine sport to one of Switzerland’s most celebrated mountain destinations.

The programme will feature the sport’s major disciplines, including downhill, Super-G, giant slalom and slalom, alongside team events. Highlights include the women’s downhill on 6 February, the men’s downhill on 7 February, and the final slalom races on 13 and 14 February.

More Than a Championship

Beyond the competition, Crans-Montana will take on a festive atmosphere throughout the Championships. Medal ceremonies, concerts, fan experiences and après-ski events will accompany the racing, while the resort itself offers exceptional skiing, mountain dining and spectacular Alpine scenery.

It is an opportunity to combine the excitement of world-class sport with a winter escape in one of Switzerland’s most celebrated mountain destinations.
