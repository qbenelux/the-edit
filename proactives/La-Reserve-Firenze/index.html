La Réserve Firenze

A Private Residence in the Heart of Florence

This October, La Réserve Firenze introduces a more intimate way to experience the Tuscan capital. Set within a beautifully restored 15th-century palazzo in Oltrarno, La Réserve Firenze offers just six private apartments. Each combines the privacy of a Florentine residence with thoughtful service, moments from the city’s cultural treasures.

Renaissance Grandeur, Reimagined

Restored by renowned interior design duo Gilles & Boissier, the palazzo brings Renaissance character together with contemporary elegance. Original frescoes and historic details give each apartment its distinctive character, while refined interiors add a sense of understated luxury.

The six residences range from an intimate one-bedroom apartment to a three-bedroom duplex with a 55 m² private terrace, each with its own character and connection to the city.

The Privacy of a Residence

Here, the independence of a private apartment is complemented by attentive service. A butler, housekeeper and concierge are available throughout the stay, while a private chef can prepare dinner at home using ingredients sourced from the nearby Santo Spirito market.

Guests can also retreat to the library and garden patio or enjoy an evening aperitivo on the shared terrace overlooking Santo Spirito and the Pitti Palace.

Florence, at Your Own Pace

Step outside and the galleries, boutiques and hidden addresses of Oltrarno are close at hand. Return, and La Réserve Firenze becomes your own quiet corner of the city — private, elegant and unmistakably Florentine.

Whether for a few days or a longer Tuscan escape, every stay can be shaped around how you wish to experience Florence.

Address: Via Santo Spirito 6, 50125 Florence, Italy
