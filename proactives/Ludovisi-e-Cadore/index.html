Ludovisi e Cadore

A New Expression of Roman Cuisine

This October, Ludovisi e Cadore introduces a new chapter in Rome’s dining scene. Behind the restaurant is Giovanni Passerini, the Roman-born chef whose years in Paris shaped a distinctive culinary style before leading him back to the flavours of his home city.

Drawing inspiration from the richness of Roman tradition, Passerini approaches familiar recipes with precision, creativity and a contemporary sensibility. His cooking remains closely connected to its origins while allowing each dish to take on a character of its own. Ludovisi e Cadore is a return to his roots, reinterpreted through years of experience beyond them.

Roman Tradition Through a Refined Lens

At Ludovisi e Cadore, Roman cuisine takes centre stage. Passerini draws particular inspiration from quinto quarto, a historic tradition deeply connected to the city's culinary identity, approaching it with delicacy and modern precision.

Dishes such as ‘trippa alla romana’ and ‘fettuccine ai regaje’ reflect this philosophy. The generosity of traditional Roman cooking meets the refined sensibility Passerini developed during his years in Paris, bringing a fresh perspective to familiar flavours while remaining true to their origins.

A Cinematic Setting in the Heart of Rome

Set within Experimental Roma, moments from Via Veneto and Villa Borghese, Ludovisi e Cadore offers an intimate setting for experiencing Rome at its most elegant. Designed by Rodolphe Parente, the interiors combine warm tones and refined details, creating an atmosphere that feels sophisticated yet unmistakably Roman.

Whether for an intimate dinner or as part of your next stay in the Eternal City, allow us to arrange your table and take care of every detail for an effortless evening in Rome.

Address: Ludovisi e Cadore – Experimental Roma, Via Veneto, Rome
